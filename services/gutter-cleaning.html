Why Is Gutter Cleaning Important?

Clogged gutters can overflow during heavy rainfall. Overflowing water may contribute to staining, erosion and moisture problems around a building's exterior.

Cleaning gutters helps maintain unobstructed drainage, although damaged or improperly installed gutter systems may require repairs beyond routine cleaning.
