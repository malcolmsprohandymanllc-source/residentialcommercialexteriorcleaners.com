Roof Cleaning Methods Depend on the Material

Asphalt shingles, metal roofing, tile and other roofing systems have different cleaning requirements. We consider manufacturer instructions, roof age, existing damage and safe access before recommending work.

Cleaning solutions are selected for compatibility with the roofing system. Landscaping protection, drainage, chemical runoff and wastewater controls are assessed before treatment. Work proceeds only when an appropriate, compliant cleaning method is available.

Why Address Roof Moss and Algae?

Organic buildup can affect the appearance of a roof and may retain moisture. A professional assessment can help determine whether cleaning or other maintenance is appropriate.

Cleaning cannot repair damaged roofing materials, and treatment results vary with the roof's condition and the type of growth.
